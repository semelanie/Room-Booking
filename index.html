Important Information

Booking Guidelines

Before You Book

01

Advance Booking

Requests must be submitted at least 48 hours before the intended date and time, to allow time for review and approval.

02

Approval Process

All bookings are reviewed by facility management. You'll receive an email once your request is approved, rejected, or needs changes.

03

Cancellation Policy

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance. Repeated late cancellations may affect future booking approvals.

04

No-Shows

Confirmed bookings that go unused without prior cancellation are logged and may be considered when reviewing future requests.

05

Room Care & Equipment

Please leave the room as you found it. Report any equipment issues right away, and note any AV or catering needs when submitting your request.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out through the contact details above.

How far in advance do I need to submit a booking?

Requests should be submitted at least 48 hours before the date and time you need the room, so there's enough time for review and approval.

How will I know if my booking has been approved?

You'll receive an email once your request has been reviewed, letting you know whether it's been approved, rejected, or needs changes. You can also check its status any time under "My Bookings".

Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?

Yes — please cancel or request a reschedule at least 24 hours before your booking. Repeated late cancellations may affect approval of future requests.

Who can request a room booking?

NIHSS staff, students, and partner organizations can all submit booking requests through this site. If you're unsure whether your event qualifies, contact us before booking.

Can I request catering or AV equipment for my booking?

Yes — note any catering, AV, or equipment needs in the "Purpose" field when you submit your request, and our team will confirm availability along with your booking.

What happens if I don't show up for a confirmed booking?

Unused bookings that weren't cancelled in advance are logged, and repeated no-shows may be taken into account when reviewing future requests.
